<summary><b>💡 Phantom Wake: SAR-Based Vessel Wake Detection for Maritime Surveillance (Click to expand)</b></summary>

### Overview
Developed a maritime surveillance solution for detecting vessel wakes in Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) data to support the identification of anomalous fishing vessels.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💡 DiaSight: AI-Powered Diabetic Retinopathy Risk Stratification (Click to expand)</b></summary>

### Overview
Led development of a non-invasive tool that uses routine clinical data to identify patients at risk of diabetic retinopathy.

### 🏆 Key Achievements
- ✅ Demonstrated strong performance, correctly identifying cases with close to 90% overall accuracy and reliably detecting both early-stage and severe cases.
- 🔍 Designed with explainable AI features to provide clear, trustworthy risk scores for clinicians.
- 🌍 Supports SDG 3 (Good Health & Well-Being) by enabling affordable, scalable screening in under-resourced communities.
- 📢 Showcased at national and international conferences, hackathons, and startup challenges.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💡 National Medical Admissions and Licensure Analysis (Click to expand)</b></summary>

### Overview
Engineered and validated an end-to-end analytics dataset covering 178,927 NMAT records, integrating assessment, university, citizenship, and PLE outcome data.

### 🏆 Key Achievements
- 📊 Recomputed authoritative scores from eight subtests after detecting a 42.2% error rate in stored raw-score totals; retained validation fields for reproducibility and auditability.
- 📈 Built deterministic PLE linkage and statistical-analysis workflows to assess performance trends, educational backgrounds, repeat-taking behavior, and licensure outcomes.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💡 DiaRisk: Surgical Risk Classification Using Fuzzy Logic (Click to expand)</b></summary>

### Overview
Built a fuzzy inference system on a 5,288-patient diabetes dataset, implementing 70 clinical rules to provide perioperative risk scores that improve surgical decision-making.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💡 RenalSight: Neuro-Symbolic CKD Risk Stratification Model (Click to expand)</b></summary>

### Overview
Integrated KDIGO-inspired clinical rules with XGBoost for robust CKD risk prediction, applying SMOTE-ENC and Optuna tuning to address class imbalance and uncertainty.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💡 OncoVista: Interpretable Deep Learning for Breast Cancer Screening (Click to expand)</b></summary>

### Overview
Designed a multi-expert EfficientNet framework for mammogram and ultrasound images.

### 🏆 Key Achievements
- 🔍 Used Grad-CAM and attention visualization for interpretable cancer classification.
- 🏥 Addresses priority cluster pain point: breast cancer detection and survival rates.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💡 DBM Infrastructure Intelligence Dashboard (Click to expand)</b></summary>

### Overview
Developed a BI dashboard for ₱6.3B worth of SUC infrastructure projects, integrating ML models to predict delays and improve accountability in government spending.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>💡 PHIVOLCS: Physics-Informed Neural Network for Earthquake Prediction (Click to expand)</b></summary>

### Overview
Built a PINN model on 113k+ earthquake records to predict peak ground acceleration (R² ≈ 0.88), embedding attenuation and magnitude-scaling laws for disaster risk reduction.

> I build machine learning systems for healthcare with a focus on explainability. A highly accurate model is useless in a clinical setting if doctors can't trust how it makes decisions. My goal is to develop tools that are transparent, rigorous, and directly useful for improving patient care—bridging the gap between raw data and actual medical workflows.
